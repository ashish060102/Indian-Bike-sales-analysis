This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Indian bike sales and resale trends across brands, fuel types, states, time periods, and sellers.
The objective is to derive actionable business insights for pricing strategy, resale value prediction, fuel-type comparison, and market demand trends.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

Understand brand-wise and model-wise resale value

Compare Petrol vs Hybrid bikes

Analyze state-wise pricing patterns

Track price and fuel trends over time

Identify top-performing bike models

📊 Key Metrics

Total Bikes Analyzed: 2,471

Total Market Price: ₹551.9 Million

Total Resale Value: ₹329.9 Million

Fuel Mix:

Petrol: 75.03%

Hybrid: 24.97%

🛠 Tools & Technologies

Tableau Desktop – Data visualization & dashboard design

Excel / CSV – Data cleaning & preprocessing

Calculated Fields – Depreciation & pricing logic

📉 Business Logic Applied

Petrol bike depreciation: 10–15% per year

Hybrid bike depreciation:

20% in first year

10% annually thereafter

🎯 Key Insights

Hybrid bikes deliver higher mileage and better resale value

Dealer-sold bikes generally retain higher resale prices

Premium models dominate top resale rankings

State-wise pricing varies significantly, indicating regional demand differences

Overall bike prices show gradual decline with minor recovery periods
